Pizza on the Grill Recipe

Description

Grilled pizza is a fun and easy way to make pizza at home. The grill gives the pizza a nice smoky flavor and a crispy crust. You can use any toppings you like, but some of my favorites are fresh mozzarella, tomatoes, basil, and olive oil.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Olive oil
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Salt and pepper
  • Optional toppings: pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, olives, etc.

Instructions

  1. Preheat your grill to medium-high heat.
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Brush one side of the dough with olive oil.
  4. Place the dough, oiled side down, on the grill. Cook for 2-3 minutes, until grill marks appear.
  5. Brush the top side with olive oil, then flip the dough over.
  6. Quickly add the tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and any other toppings you like.
  7. Close the grill lid and cook for another 3-5 minutes, until the cheese is melted and the crust is crispy.
  8. Remove the pizza from the grill and top with fresh basil leaves. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  9. Slice and serve immediately. Enjoy!
